Each talking board gives you key phrases with full kanji, kana, and English. Use the dropdowns to substitute places and numbers, build a conversation script, and listen to every phrase with text-to-speech.

Ask where places are, find out how far away they are, and understand instructions for turning right, turning left, and going straight ahead.

Ask which platform your train departs from, find out how long the journey takes, and get instructions for changing lines on Tokyo's rail network.

03🚌バスに乗りますバスにのりますRiding a Bus

Confirm your bus route, ask where to get off for your destination, check the fare, and know what to say when you need to press the stop button.

Tell the driver your destination, ask for an estimated fare and travel time, request a receipt, make conversation en route, and understand the taxi light signs.
